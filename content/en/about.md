# About

> Hugo is one of the most popular open-source static site generators. With its amazing speed and flexibility, Hugo makes building websites fun again.

Ferro is a Hugo blog theme forked from [Rusty Typewriter](https://github.com/math-queiroz/rusty-typewriter) by [DangPang](https://github.com/dangpangch). It keeps the retro typewriter character of the original while adding built-in bilingual support (English & Simplified Chinese), per-language search indexes, a language switcher, and data-driven side pane content. It's designed to stay simple and suitable for personal blogs — feel free to use it if you find it worth it.
